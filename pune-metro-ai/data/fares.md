# Pune Metro Fare Matrix

Fares are one-way, in ₹. For a same-station entry, fare is 0.

Source: Pune Metro's official English fare chart (`Fare-Chart-E-Web.avif`),
verified against the live passenger-information page on 11 August 2026. Fare values
are directional lookups exactly as printed; do not estimate or reverse a station pair.

The official chart prints **District Court / CVC** twice: once on the Purple Line and once on the Aqua Line. The table uses `CVC (Purple)` and `CVC (Aqua)` only to distinguish those two otherwise identical printed labels.
