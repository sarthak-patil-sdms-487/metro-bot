# Pune Metro Passenger Rules

> The confirmed guidance below is paraphrased from Pune Metro's Commuters Guide. The official prohibited-items PDF is a scanned image and still needs manual transcription or OCR.

## Luggage

- Keep personal luggage manageable and make sure it does not block doors, aisles, or other passengers.
- Take care that loose items such as sarees, dupattas, and bags do not create a risk while boarding or alighting.
- Follow station staff directions when carrying larger items.

## Food and drink

> Unverified/inferred: the official Commuters Guide does not explicitly state a food or drink policy. Keep stations and trains clean and dispose of waste properly.

## Ticketing and travel

- Do not jump over AFC gates or otherwise bypass the normal entry and exit process.
- Do not share a smart card or token with another person.
- Never force train doors open or try to enter or leave while doors are closing.
- Queue where required, follow station staff instructions, and allow passengers to leave the train before boarding.
- Mind the gap between the platform and train while entering or exiting.

## Accessibility

- Lifts and priority facilities marked for persons with disabilities, senior citizens, and sick persons are reserved for those riders; do not use them when you do not need them.
- Reserved seats are intended for elderly passengers, women, and riders with disabilities.

## Commuter Etiquette

- Let passengers alight before boarding, and do not block feeder bus or train entry.
- Give up reserved seats for elderly passengers, women, and passengers with disabilities.
- Do not spit; keep noise and phone calls low; do not litter.
- Do not carry firearms, jump AFC gates, share a smart card or token, force train doors open, or misuse PWD or senior-citizen lifts.
