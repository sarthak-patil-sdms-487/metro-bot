# One Pune Card (Non-KYC / MTS)

## Discounts

- Receive a 10% discount on weekdays.
- Receive a 30% discount on weekends.

## Fees and top-up

- Issuance fee: ₹50 plus tax.
- Annual maintenance fee: ₹75 plus tax.
- Minimum top-up: ₹50.
- Top-up fee: 1.8%, capped at ₹12.

## Balance and use restrictions

- Maximum total balance: ₹3,000.
- Maximum chip balance: ₹2,000.
- The card cannot be used for retail, POS, or e-commerce payments.
- Cash withdrawal, refunds, and transfers are not available.
