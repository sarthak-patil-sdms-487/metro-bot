# One Pune Card (KYC)

## Fees and validity

- Issuance fee: ₹100.
- Annual maintenance fee: ₹75.
- Replacement fee: ₹100.
- The card is valid for 3 years from issuance.

## Balance and top-up

- Maximum total balance: ₹2,000.
- Minimum top-up: ₹50.
- Top-up fee: 1.8%, capped at ₹12.
- Balance enquiry is free.

## Use and limits

- Use the card contactlessly by tapping in and tapping out.
- Maximum value per transaction: ₹2,000.
- Transaction limit: 20 transactions per day.

## KYC limits

- Limited KYC cards may be loaded up to ₹10,000 per month and must be upgraded to Full KYC within 2 years.
- For Full KYC, the balance cannot exceed ₹2,00,000.

## Lost or stolen cards

- Call 022-45000800 immediately to report a lost or stolen card.
- The chip balance is non-refundable.
